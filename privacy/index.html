Privacy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The archive keeps clothes, references and the people who make them. It does not keep tabs on you. Here is exactly what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, how long it stays, and how to see or delete it.

The short version

  • Analytics only if you say yes. We use Google Analytics cookies to see which parts of the archive people reach, but only after you tap "Accept analytics". No ads, no ad pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • We never sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we never will.
  • We only collect what you give us: your email if you join the list, your entry if you enter the Casting Room, and what you do with the Discord bot.
  • A few small things are saved on your own device so the site remembers you. You can clear them below in one tap.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time. In Discord, /my-data does it instantly.

3. Cookies and on-device storage

Analytics cookies are set only if you allow them. When you first visit, a popup asks whether we may use Google Analytics. Until you tap "Accept analytics", Google's analytics script does not load and no analytics cookie is set. "Decline" is just as easy, the site works exactly the same either way, and you can change your mind any time from a "Cookie settings" link or the panel below. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we treat that as a no and never ask. We ask again after 12 months.

4. The Discord server and the Archivist

Discord stores your account and your messages under its own privacy policy. Our bot, the Archivist, keeps a small record on our contest server (hosted by Cloudflare):

  • Your Discord user ID and username, your personal invite link, how many people joined through it, which hidden words you found, and whether you opted in to bump reminders.
  • Your Passport number and stamps, and the casting files you post with /casting-file (for the Friday roll call).
  • Collab calls you post with /crew-call live in Discord like any other message.

The Archivist is an AI character. When you use a talking command (/ask, /clue, /casefile, /reframe, /icebreaker, /observe, /read-the-room or "Ask the Archivist"), what you type goes to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. /observe also sends your own recent messages in that channel, /read-the-room sends the channel's recent messages, and "Ask the Archivist" sends the message you pointed it at. For /read-the-room, names are replaced with labels like "Member A" before anything is sent. We keep your last few exchanges for up to 2 days so the Archivist can follow a conversation, then delete them automatically. Do not send the Archivist anything private. Its answers can be wrong, and it never judges contest entries.

The Sunday fit pick reads reaction counts on posts in #fit-archive. The bot does not read or store other channel messages.

See or erase it yourself: type /my-data in the server to see what the bot keeps about you, and /my-data delete:true to erase it.

5. What is public by design

  • Your contest entry is a public post on Instagram or TikTok, and we may repost it with credit as the official rules describe.
  • Casting files, collab calls, Passport stamp announcements, editorial features and the Friday roll call are visible to members of the Discord server.
  • A Guest Pass page (punxarmy.com/pass/…) shows the entrant's handle and the crew member's handle to anyone who has the link. These pages are not listed in search engines, and an unclaimed pass stops showing either handle after 21 days.
  • A Passport page (punxarmy.com/passport/…) shows its stamps and handle to anyone with the Passport number. It is not listed in search engines.

8. Your rights and choices

  • See it: ask what we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Fix it: ask us to correct anything wrong.
  • Delete it: ask us to delete it. In Discord, /my-data delete:true. A contest entry can be withdrawn by email; once judging has started, a withdrawn entry is simply not judged.
  • Stop emails: every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Take back consent at any time, without affecting anything done before.

Email booking@punxarmy.com with the subject "Privacy request". We may ask you to confirm the request comes from you (for example, from the account or email involved), and we answer within 30 days, or within 45 days where state law allows. Exercising a right never changes how your contest entry is judged.

9. California and other US states

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon or another state with a consumer privacy law, you have the rights in section 8, and:

  • We do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the past 12 months. Google Analytics runs only with your consent and with all advertising features off. We honor Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends it, analytics stays off.
  • In the past 12 months we collected these categories, for the purposes in section 2: identifiers (handles, email, Discord ID, hashed address); internet activity (request data, bot command use, and site usage through Google Analytics for visitors who allow it); and audio-visual content you choose to submit (your entry post). We do not use sensitive personal information to infer anything about you.
  • We will not discriminate against you for using your rights. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask the agent for proof of authorization.
  • If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; we respond to appeals within 60 days.

10. Visitors outside the US

The contest is open only to US residents, but anyone can visit the site and join the server. We are based in the United States and our providers process data in the US and other countries. If you are in the EU, the UK or another region with data protection law, you have the rights in section 8, plus the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. Where required, our providers transfer data under recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Children

PUNX ARMY is not directed to children under 13, and Discord does not allow users under 13. The contest and all collaboration channels are for adults 18 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

12. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with strict security headers, contest entries are rate-limited and checked for automated submissions, connection addresses are stored only as salted one-way hashes, and every request to the Discord bot is signature-checked. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change matters, we will also post it in the Discord #announcements channel and, if you are on the email list, tell you by email before it takes effect.
