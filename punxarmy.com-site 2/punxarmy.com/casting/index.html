How it works

  1. Wear the truth. Not the fit you think wins: the one that's actually you. The jacket you saved for. Your grandmother's ring. The shoes you wore down walking your city. One 10–20 second vertical video or 3–5 photos. A phone is enough. It just has to be impossible to mistake for anyone else.
  2. Send the signal. Post it publicly on Instagram or TikTok with @punxarmy, #PUNXContest and the words "contest entry". The feed flattens people; this is how you stand out of it. And somewhere, someone who dresses like nobody around them sees yours and finally posts theirs.
  3. Put it on the record. Fill in the form below (about two minutes). Your look is filed in ARCHIVE 001, and you get your free PUNX Passport with its first stamp.
  4. Take your people with you. Credit the photographer, the stylist, the friend who said "wear that." They get a Guest Pass into the Casting Room, because when your look gets seen, so do the people who got you there.

Entries: Oct 29, 12:00 am ET to Nov 11, 11:59 pm ET. 18+, US residents. Up to two looks each.

You're not just answering a casting call. You're building something.

The Flattening wants everyone to look the same, post the same and scroll past each other. The Casting Room is the opposite: a lookbook made by the people in it. There is no ARCHIVE 001 without the people who fill it. Every look, every crew credit, every reference, every "welcome in" said to someone new becomes part of the record of real, specific people who refused to be made identical.

Only one look wins the shoot. But every person who walks in makes this bigger, and the people who show up first are the ones who decide what it becomes. That's you.

The prompt

SHOW US A LOOK THAT FEELS LIKE YOU.
No designer clothes needed. No pro shoot needed. No follower count needed. Just a point of view, and the people who helped you get there.

Show your style, where you are (your city, room, street, mirror), and one detail worth noticing: a texture, a shoe, a garment, your hair, jewelry, posture, makeup, tailoring, a found object. Pick a starting point, or ignore them all:

01
The first look

The outfit you'd wear if people were seeing you for the first time.

02
Off-duty / on-signal

A real-life look elevated by one intentional choice.

03
The detail that makes it yours

One garment, shoe, piece of jewelry, hairstyle, makeup detail, layer or texture.

04
City uniform

A look shaped by where you're from or where you move through.

05
Borrowed / built / found

Something thrifted, altered, borrowed, handmade or meaningful.

06
Before the room opens

A getting-ready, mirror, street-walk, arrival or movement video.

You never have to "dress like PUNX". PUNX documents real people's style.

Enter as who you became

At the end of the initiation, the archive gave you a role. In the Casting Room, it becomes your direction.

Every role is a way of seeing a look: a line to live by, a direction for your tape, and one signature shot to land. Use it, bend it, or ignore it completely. It's a starting point, never a rule, and the Reading Panel never scores it. Haven't been initiated? Take the 7 minutes and find out who you are in this world, or just pick the one that sounds like you.

SOUND · SELECTOR

“You know when a record needs room.”

Every fit has a tempo.

Direction: Let the look move to a sound. Cut on the beat; let the outfit be the track.

Signature shot: The drop: something lands, a hand, a step, a sleeve, exactly on the beat.

FORM · FORMMAKER

“You turn fragments into identity.”

Pieces on a floor. Then, somehow, you.

Direction: Show the look before it’s a look. The pile, the pin, the choice.

Signature shot: The last piece goes on, and suddenly it’s a person.

MOTION · MOTIONKEEPER

“You keep the city moving.”

Clothes were made to go somewhere.

Direction: Never stand still. One take, one walk, the look in motion.

Signature shot: The pass: you walk past the camera, not to it.

PLACE · CITY SCRIBE

“You know your city has a memory.”

Wear the street you came from.

Direction: Put the look where it belongs: the block, the bus stop, the corner store that’s been there forever.

Signature shot: The sign: somewhere in the frame, a word only your city would know.

MEMORY · MEMORYKEEPER

“You don’t let the important parts disappear.”

Some things are worn. Some things are kept.

Direction: Build the look around something with history. Whose was it? Where has it been?

Signature shot: The hands: close on the piece, held like it matters, because it does.

RELAY · SIGNAL RELAY

“You connect people to what matters.”

Nobody gets restored alone.

Direction: Make it with someone. Shoot your crew, swap a piece, pass the camera.

Signature shot: The handoff: the frame goes from you to them.

The Restoration the prize

The Flattening erases people one identical post at a time. The Restoration puts one back, and this time it's permanent.

Most prizes end when the shoot wraps. This one makes you part of the story.

I · THE SHOOT
A campaign built around you

A paid PUNX editorial with a real crew, styled around your look. You walk away with 10–15 edited photographs and a vertical campaign film, yours to use with credit. The kind of work most people wait years to have in their portfolio.

II · CANON
You become part of the archive. Forever.

Your look gets its own room at the end of the PUNX initiation. Every person who walks through punxarmy.com, tonight and years from now, finishes the journey at your file. Then the Archivist writes you into the lore as an official Transmission, a chapter of the PUNX story with your name on it.

III · THE CHARACTER
The world rebuilds you.

Your look is rebuilt, piece by piece, as a character in the PUNX universe: modeled in 3D in the same world as the film, and walking through a future chapter or campaign visual. Your face stays yours; it's your look that becomes legend, unless you want to be fully in it.

IV · THE FOUNDING FILE
Number 001

The PUNX PASSPORT // FOUNDING FILE, numbered 001 and the only one that will ever exist, plus Tape 001 when it's released, and a feature across PUNX Instagram, Discord and punxarmy.com.

The face of Archive 001 doesn't just get photographed. They get kept.

The Reading Panel how it's judged

Three readers, one PUNX lead and two independent fashion creatives, read every eligible file against the same rubric. They read files, not follower counts.

CriterionWeight
Personal point of view35%
Styling and visual direction25%
Presence: how the look is carried20%
Story behind the look15%
Execution (phone quality is fine)5%
Never scored: follower count, likes, views, comments, purchases, Crew Credits, Guest Passes, Discord activity, reactions or popularity of any kind.

Crew Credit

Nobody makes a look alone. Name one person who helped make yours possible: the photographer, stylist, makeup or hair artist, designer, the friend who held the phone. They get a Guest Pass into the Casting Room with their name on it, and when they walk in you both get a Passport stamp. It's the simplest way to say "you mattered to this" in public.

Crew Credit is a community-recognition feature. It does not affect judging, does not add entries, does not increase your chances of winning, and does not require a purchase.
