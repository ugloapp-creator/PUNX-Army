Anyone can visit the site. To join the Discord server you must meet Discord's minimum age (13 in the US). The Casting Room contest and every collaboration channel are for adults 18 and older, and the contest is limited to US residents as the official rules say. Everyone else is still welcome in the room: talk fashion, share references, answer the Studio Calls, listen in on the Stage.

4. What you make stays yours

You keep ownership of everything you create and share with us: entries, casting files, references, messages and photos. When you post something in the Discord server, you let us display it inside the server, and pin, feature or credit it there. Contest entries come with the limited reposting license in the official rules. Any other use, such as advertising, print, merchandise or a campaign film, needs your separate written permission and the permission of everyone shown. Ask us to take down a repost and we will, within 72 hours.

Only share what you have the right to share. Credit your sources and your crew. Don't post images of other people without their permission.

5. Room rules

The archive is for everyone who loves clothes and the people who make them, at every budget, size, age, gender, background and level of experience. So:

  • Talk about the work, never the body. Comments on anyone's body, face, gender, age, race, religion, disability or budget are removed.
  • No harassment, hate, threats or bullying, in public channels or in DMs that start here.
  • No sexual content, and nothing sexual involving anyone under 18. Ever. No nude or lingerie requests in collab channels.
  • No spam, scams, fake engagement, follow-for-follow, vote trading or advertising other servers without permission.
  • Credit what isn't yours. No scraped or uncredited images of real people.
  • Don't try to break things: no attempts to get around moderation, the bot's limits or the contest's rules.

Moderators may remove content, take away roles, or remove anyone from the server who breaks these rules. Report anything that feels off to a moderator, or email us.

6. Collabs, trades and meeting up

The looking-for channels, crew calls and #the-exchange are for members to find each other. We don't vet members and can't vouch for anyone. Meet in public places or bring a friend, agree pay or credit in writing before a shoot, only work with adults, and use payment methods with buyer protection for trades. Arrangements between members are between those members, and PUNX is not responsible for them.

The Archivist is an AI character run by PUNX, not a person. Its answers are generated by an AI model and can be wrong, out of date or incomplete. It is not professional, legal, financial, medical or mental-health advice. It cannot authenticate garments or promise prices, and it never judges the contest. Don't share private or sensitive information with it. If you are ever in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, contact 988 in the US or your local emergency number. How your messages to it are handled is explained in the privacy policy.
