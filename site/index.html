RIGHTS & CREDITS

READ BEFORE LAUNCH
  • Recordings. Two mixes — MESS IT UP (MIX DG18) and GEMS (MIX DG7) — are served from this site and play as its background music, supplied by the artist for this campaign. They are re‑encoded to 128 kbps for delivery; the masters are not distributed here. Nothing plays until the visitor turns sound on.
  • The catalogue is metadata only. None of the eleven released transmissions is hosted, streamed or cached by this page. Their playback works solely from audio files the operator loads from their own device, which stay in the browser for the session and are never uploaded. Every listening link points at YESOP's official platform pages.
  • Sound made by this page. Every sound in it — the plastic clack of a figure, the paper turn, the rubber stamp, the shutter, the polaroid motor, the dog tags, the boots, the drill cadence and the rift — is synthesised live by this page's own oscillator and noise code. There is no sample in any of it, of a recording or of anything else, and no third-party audio file is loaded.
  • The figures. Every soldier is drawn from scratch by this page's own code — no photograph, sprite sheet, model or clip art of a toy soldier is used, and none of the reference images sent during design were copied into it. Generic plastic army figures are a decades-old commodity form, not anyone's trademark; nothing here imitates a specific manufacturer's branding, packaging or characters.
  • Imagery. All photography, artwork, garments and the PUNX wordmark belong to the artist. Photography by YESOP. No stock or scraped images.
  • Fiction. The crossing, the campaign, the field notes and the dialogue are fiction written for this campaign. Nothing in them is a statement, quote or lyric by the artist. Anything factual — titles, release years, lengths, credits — comes from the official EPK and is labelled as such.
  • No invented audience. No follower counts, listener numbers or community tallies appear anywhere. Every figure in the formation is a real person in your room, and every number on screen reflects something you or they actually did.
  • Nothing military is being sold. The army is a metaphor for an audience arriving together. No figure in this world carries a weapon — they carry cameras, boomboxes, spray cans, crates and colours — and no real conflict, force or service is depicted, invoked or endorsed.
  • Data. Your record — figure, rank, march and service history — lives in this browser's local storage. No account, no analytics, no cookies, no personal data collected, and destroying the record erases all of it.
  • What the muster sends. While you are in a room, and only then, this page shares your chosen name, figure colour, ready state and the lanes you strike with the other figures in that room, so the drill can be scored. On the standard build that traffic never leaves your own machine — it passes between browser tabs. Where a room server is configured, it passes to that server instead. Chat is relayed to the room and never stored. Leave the room and the sharing stops.
  • Payments. There is no subscription, no fee, no membership and no donation link anywhere in this app. Nothing in the game is behind a price. The quartermaster is the only place money can move, it can only offer real items from a real shop, and where no shop is set up there is nothing to buy and no way to reach one. Where it is open, payment happens on the provider's own hosted pages: this site never handles, sees or stores card details, and buying nothing costs you no part of the game.
